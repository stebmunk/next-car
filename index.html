Decision notes

Where I'm headed

A fun daily with the stance of an old sport wagon, the driver assist of a new car, and a price that lets the first owner eat the steep depreciation.

Last updated October 23, 2026

01

Priorities

  1. Highest
    Driving fun and a sport-wagon feel

    The car has to be something I want on an empty on-ramp. The long roof and the way an Avant or a 3 Series wagon sits are the reference.

  2. Highest
    Top tech and driver assist

    Lane tracing and traffic-jam assist sit on the same shelf as the driving. This is a tie with the line above, ahead of everything else.

  3. Next
    Cost, after someone else took the depreciation

    Target is a roughly two-year-old car, the fast-depreciating kind, so the purchase price has already done a lot of falling.

  4. Required
    AWD

    All-wheel drive is mandatory. Rear-drive and front-drive cars are logged when they show up, then set aside.

  5. If EV
    Road-trip range, and 800V charging when I can get it

    An EV has to cover a real trip. 800-volt charging, with a short 10–80% stop, is the architecture I want. A 400-volt car can still win on how it drives.

The reference

  • Audi A4 Avant
  • Audi A6 Avant
  • Audi allroad
  • BMW 3 Series wagon
